03 — PROCESS METHODOLOGY FIVE-STAGE WORKFLOW

FROM IDEA
TO FINAL
VISUAL.

01

UNDERSTAND

Understand the objective, audience and message before putting pen to paper or pixels to canvas.

02

EXPLORE

Research references, typography, imagery and creative directions to discover fresh visual angles.

03

CREATE

Develop the visual concept and composition, establishing strong hierarchies and dynamic tension.

04

REFINE

Improve hierarchy, spacing, contrast and visual details until every element works in harmony.

05

DELIVER

Prepare the final artwork for its intended platform with production-ready precision and consistency.
